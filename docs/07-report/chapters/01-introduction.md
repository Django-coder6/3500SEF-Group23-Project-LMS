# 1. The Problem and the Context

Community collection points have become the last mile of online shopping: couriers deliver in bulk, residents collect in person. Staff at a point handle two problems a paper log cannot solve — finding a free shelf slot of the right size, and proving who took which parcel when. A survey of 29 residents and staff (`survey-results.md`) put lost or mis-filed parcels and handover disputes at the top of the list. This project builds the system that records a parcel's location, its pickup code and every handover, for one collection point.
