# 1. The Problem and the Context

As the final step of e-commerce delivery, community collection points receive bulk parcels from couriers for local residents to pick up. However, current tracking methods rely on scattered spreadsheets, chat histories, and personal memory. As a result, staff cannot reliably answer two critical questions: where a parcel is currently located, and who collected it.

According to two questionnaires (29 responses in total, documented in `survey-results.md`), the primary pain points differ by role. Residents primarily struggle with delayed or missing notifications (4 out of 11), while staff are most hindered by batch registration delays (4 out of 7). 

To address these inefficiencies, this project develops a management system that accurately records parcel locations, generates secure pickup codes, and logs every handover event at the collection point.
