# 1. The Problem and the Context

Community collection points receive bulk parcels from couriers for local residents to pick up. Staff already use digital tools to manage these parcels: among the seven staff respondents, three rely on spreadsheets, two use existing software, and two use chat histories. Because these records are fragmented, staff cannot reliably answer two critical questions: where a parcel is currently located, and who collected it.

Two questionnaires (`survey-results.md`, 29 responses) put a different problem first on each side of the counter: among 11 residents, notification arriving late or not at all (4 of 11); among 7 staff, batch registration (4 of 7).

This project develops a management system that records parcel locations, generates pickup codes, and logs every handover event at the collection point.
