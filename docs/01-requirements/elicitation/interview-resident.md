## interview dialogue (five pre-planned questions)

### Q1. Walk me through your most recent parcel pickup from arrival to collection.

**Fictional participant:** I got a message with a pickup code in the afternoon, and I went to the collection point around 7 p.m. I gave the code to the employee. They looked on two different shelves before finding the parcel, and then they marked it as collected. The actual handover was quick, but waiting while they searched felt slow.

### Q2. How long does pickup usually take? Which step causes the longest wait?

**Fictional participant:** When there is no queue it can take two or three minutes. In the evening, I might wait around eight minutes. Most of that time is either queuing or watching staff search for parcels on crowded shelves.

### Q3. Have you experienced a missing parcel or unclear notification? How was it resolved?

**Fictional participant:** Once I received a collection message, but staff could not find the parcel straight away. They checked the tracking number again and found it under a different shelf label. I would prefer the notification to show clearly whether the parcel is ready for collection.

### Q4. Have friends or relatives collected parcels on your behalf? How was permission verified?

**Fictional participant:** My flatmate has collected a parcel for me. I forwarded the pickup code in a chat message. I was not sure if the same code could be used again, so I would rather share an authorization that expires automatically after pickup.

### Q5. If you could improve just one part of pickup, what would it be?

**Fictional participant:** Please make parcel lookup faster. If the system tells staff the exact shelf and confirms the parcel status, that would probably cut both the waiting time and the confusion.

## Illustrative analysis — not validated findings

**Potential pain points:**
